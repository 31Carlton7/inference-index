Nebius Token Factory Sub-processors
Copy MCP ServerCopy MCP Server
Copy MCP ServerCopy MCP Server
This page identifies the sub-processors engaged by Nebius to process Customer Personal Data on behalf of Customers in connection with Nebius Token Factory under the applicable Data Processing Agreement.
The sub-processors relevant to a Customer depend on the Token Factory functionality used, the model or endpoint selected, the applicable processing location and, where relevant, whether the Customer requests support.
For purposes of this list, a Nebius Group entity is included only where it is engaged by the Nebius contracting entity to process Customer Personal Data on its behalf. The role of the Nebius contracting entity itself as a processor or sub-processor depends on the Customer’s role under the applicable DPA.
Service providers that process personal data for Nebius’ own purposes as a controller, rather than Customer Personal Data on behalf of Customers, are not included in this list.
Infrastructure and inference
The sub-processors applicable to a Customer depend on the model, endpoint type and processing location used. For public model endpoints, the current processing location is displayed for each model in the Token Factory interface. For dedicated endpoints, processing takes place in the region selected by the Customer.
