Data Processing Addendum
This Data Processing Addendum (the “DPA”) forms part of the Terms of Service (“ToS”) and any other agreement governing the direct relationship between the Parties relating to the Services (the ToS and any other agreement collectively, the “Agreement”) by and between SambaNova Systems, Inc. (“SambaNova”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) and you, whether personally or on behalf of an entity (“Customer”) (each individually a “Party” and collectively the “Parties”). This DPA applies to SambaNova’s Processing of Customer Personal Data in connection with the Services and becomes effective when Customer accepts or otherwise becomes bound by the Agreement.
Definitions. Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.
“Adequacy Decision” means a legally binding decision or regulation issued under applicable Data Protection Laws recognizing that a country, territory, sector, or recipient provides an adequate level of protection for Personal Data.
“Affiliate” means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the subject entity.
“Controller” means the entity that determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data and includes a “business” or equivalent regulated entity to the extent that term applies under Data Protection Laws.
“Customer Personal Data” means Personal Data Processed by SambaNova on behalf of Customer in connection with the Services. Customer Personal Data does not include Personal Data for which SambaNova determines the purposes and means of Processing independently of Customer.
“Data Protection Laws” means all applicable laws and legally binding regulations relating to privacy, data protection, data security, breach notification, or the Processing of Personal Data that apply to a Party’s Processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement, including, as applicable:
Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“EU GDPR”), laws of the European Economic Area (“EEA”) and their member states implementing or supplementing the EU GDPR, and Directive 2002/58/EC as implemented in applicable national law;
the United Kingdom (“UK”) GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003, in each case as amended, including by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (collectively, “UK Data Protection Laws”);
the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection of 25 September 2020 (SR 235.1) and its implementing ordinances, including the Data Protection Ordinance (SR 235.11), in each case as amended (collectively, the “FADP”); and
applicable United States federal and state privacy and data-protection laws, including, without limitation, the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, and its implementing regulations (collectively, the “CCPA”).
“Data Subject” means an identified or identifiable natural person, consumer, household, or other individual to whom Personal Data relates, as applicable under Data Protection Laws.
“EU SCCs” means the standard contractual clauses annexed to European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.
“Personal Data” means any information defined as “personal data,” “personal information,” or a substantially equivalent term under applicable Data Protection Laws.
“Processor” means an entity that Processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller and includes a “service provider,” “contractor,” or equivalent regulated entity to the extent that term applies under Data Protection Laws.
“Restricted Transfer” means a transfer of Personal Data that requires an Adequacy Decision, standard contractual clauses, contractual safeguards, regulatory approval, transfer assessment, or another transfer mechanism under Data Protection Laws.
“Security Incident” means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or unauthorized access to Customer Personal Data Processed by SambaNova or a Subprocessor. Security Incident does not include an unsuccessful attempt or activity that does not compromise the security of Customer Personal Data, such as an unsuccessful login attempt, ping, port scan, denial-of-service attack, or other network attack.
“Services” shall have the meaning ascribed to it in the Agreement.
“Subprocessor” means a third party appointed by or on behalf of SambaNova to Process Customer Personal Data.
“UK Addendum” means the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses issued under section 119A of the UK Data Protection Act 2018, as revised from time to time.
Designation.
Customer is a Controller of Customer Personal Data, or where Customer Processes Customer Personal Data on behalf of another Controller, a Processor authorized by that Controller to appoint SambaNova as a Subprocessor. Customer appoints SambaNova as a Processor or Subprocessor, as applicable, to Process Customer Personal Data solely for the purposes and subject to the documented instructions described in the Agreement, this DPA, and Annex I. Where Customer acts as a Processor, Customer represents that its instructions to SambaNova, including its appointment of SambaNova as a Subprocessor, have been authorized by the relevant Controller. Customer will provide the identity and contact information of that Controller only to the extent required by Data Protection Laws. To the extent SambaNova Processes Personal Data as an independent Controller for its own legitimate purposes, such as managing the commercial relationship or complying with its independent legal obligations, that Processing is outside the scope of this DPA and is governed by SambaNova’s applicable privacy notice and Data Protection Laws.
Customer responsibilities. Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of its Processing of Customer Personal Data and its instructions to SambaNova. Customer represents and warrants that: (i) it has provided, and will continue to provide, all notices and has established, and will continue to maintain, all valid legal bases, authorizations, permissions, and where required, consents necessary for SambaNova and its Subprocessors to lawfully Process Customer Personal Data as contemplated by the Agreement and this DPA; (ii) its collection, use, disclosure, and other Processing of Customer Personal Data, including its disclosure of Customer Personal Data to SambaNova and its Subprocessors, complies with Data Protection Laws; and (iii) its Processing instructions comply with applicable law and will not cause SambaNova to violate Data Protection Laws when SambaNova follows those instructions in accordance with this DPA. Customer will not provide SambaNova with Customer Personal Data that is unnecessary for the Services or that the Agreement expressly prohibits Customer from submitting.
Obligations.
Compliance with Law. Each Party shall comply with the obligations imposed on it by Data Protection Laws in connection with its Processing of Customer Personal Data. SambaNova shall maintain any records concerning its Processing required by Data Protection Laws and shall cooperate with competent data-protection authorities as legally required. SambaNova shall promptly notify Customer if SambaNova determines that it can no longer comply with this DPA or applicable Data Protection Laws.
Limitations on Processing. SambaNova shall Process Customer Personal Data only: (i) to provide, maintain, support, secure, and improve the Services as described in the Agreement and Annex I; (ii) in accordance with Customer’s documented lawful instructions; or (iii) as required by applicable law, in which case SambaNova shall, unless legally prohibited, inform Customer of the applicable legal requirement before Processing the Customer Personal Data. The Agreement, this DPA, Customer’s configuration and authorized use of the Services, and any other written instructions agreed by the Parties constitute Customer’s documented instructions. SambaNova shall not Process Customer Personal Data for its own advertising, independent marketing, or unrelated commercial purposes and shall not use Customer Personal Data to train or fine-tune any machine-learning or artificial-intelligence model. If SambaNova reasonably believes that an instruction violates Data Protection Laws, SambaNova shall promptly notify Customer, unless legally prohibited. Pending resolution, SambaNova may suspend the affected instruction to the extent reasonably necessary to avoid a violation of Data Protection Laws.
Anonymous and Deidentified Data. SambaNova may create and use information derived from Customer Personal Data only where that information has been aggregated, anonymized, or deidentified in accordance with Data Protection Laws so that it cannot reasonably be associated with Customer or an identified or identifiable individual. SambaNova shall: (i) implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to prevent reidentification; (ii) maintain and use the information only in aggregated, anonymous, or deidentified form; (iii) not attempt to re-identify the information or associate it with Customer or any individual; (iv) publicly commit to maintaining and using deidentified information without attempting to reidentify it, where required by the CCPA; and (v) contractually require any recipient of the information to comply with restrictions required by Data Protection Laws. SambaNova may use such information to operate, analyze, secure, and improve the Services and for other lawful internal business purposes, but not to create or augment profiles concerning identifiable individuals or to train or fine-tune any machine-learning or artificial-intelligence model.
U.S. State Privacy Requirements. To the extent Customer Personal Data is subject to the CCPA or another U.S. state privacy law and SambaNova acts as a service provider, contractor, or processor, SambaNova shall: (i) Process Customer Personal Data only for the limited and specified purposes described in the Agreement, this DPA, and Annex I; (ii) not sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined by the CCPA, or otherwise Process Customer Personal Data for targeted advertising where prohibited by applicable Data Protection Laws; (iii) not retain, use, or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Customer and SambaNova or for any commercial purpose other than the limited and specified purposes described in the Agreement and this DPA, except as otherwise permitted by Data Protection Laws; (iv) not combine Customer Personal Data received from or on behalf of Customer with Personal Data received from another person or collected through SambaNova’s own interactions with a Data Subject, except to the extent expressly permitted by Data Protection Laws and necessary to provide the Services; (v) provide the same level of privacy protection for Customer Personal Data as is required of Customer under applicable Data Protection Laws; (vi) notify Customer promptly if SambaNova determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under applicable Data Protection Laws; (vii) permit Customer, subject to Section 11, to take reasonable and appropriate steps to verify that SambaNova’s Processing is consistent with Customer’s obligations under Data Protection Laws; (viii) permit Customer to take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate any unauthorized Processing of Customer Personal Data; and (ix) certify that it understands and will comply with the restrictions and obligations set forth in this Section 4(d).
Limited and Specified Business Purposes. The limited and specific purposes for which SambaNova may Process Customer Personal Data are providing, maintaining, supporting, securing, and troubleshooting the Services; authenticating users; detecting and preventing security incidents, fraud, and unlawful activity; providing customer support; performing internal auditing and service analytics necessary to provide the Services; and undertaking internal research and development reasonably directed toward maintaining or improving the Services, in each case only as permitted by Data Protection Laws.
Data Subject Rights. If SambaNova receives a request from a Data Subject concerning Customer Personal Data, SambaNova shall promptly notify Customer and shall not respond to the request except on Customer’s documented instructions or as required by law. Taking into account the nature of the Processing, SambaNova shall provide reasonable assistance through appropriate technical and organizational measures to enable Customer to respond to requests to exercise rights under Data Protection Laws. If legally required to respond directly, SambaNova shall inform Customer before responding unless legally prohibited.
Government and Regulatory Requests. Unless legally prohibited, SambaNova shall promptly notify Customer if it receives a legally binding request from a governmental, regulatory, or judicial authority seeking disclosure of Customer Personal Data. Each Party shall reasonably cooperate with the other in responding to a request relating to the Processing of Customer Personal Data, subject to applicable law and appropriate confidentiality protections.
Cross-Border Transfers. Neither Party shall make a Restricted Transfer of Customer Personal Data unless the transfer is permitted by Data Protection Laws. The Parties may rely on an applicable Adequacy Decision or another valid transfer mechanism recognized under Data Protection Laws.
Transfers Subject to the EU GDPR. Where Customer transfers Customer Personal Data to SambaNova and the transfer is subject to Chapter V of the EU GDPR but is not covered by an Adequacy Decision or another valid transfer mechanism, the EU SCCs are incorporated into this DPA, and attached hereto as Exhibit A, and apply as follows:

The competent supervisory authority is determined in accordance with Clause 13 and Annex I.C.

Other Restricted Transfers. Where a Restricted Transfer is subject to Data Protection Laws other than the EU GDPR or UK Data Protection Laws and no Adequacy Decision or other valid transfer mechanism applies, the EU SCCs shall apply to that transfer as set out in this Section 6, with such amendments as are necessary under the relevant Data Protection Laws, including that: (a) references to the EU GDPR shall be read as references to the relevant Data Protection Laws; (b) the competent supervisory authority shall be the authority designated under the relevant Data Protection Laws; and (c) references to “Member State” shall not prevent Data Subjects from exercising their rights in their country of habitual residence in accordance with Clause 18(c). For the avoidance of doubt, this Section applies to transfers subject to the FADP, for which the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (“FDPIC”) is the competent supervisory authority. Where the relevant Data Protection Laws require a transfer mechanism other than the EU SCCs, the Parties shall reasonably cooperate to implement that mechanism, and the EU SCCs and UK Addendum shall not replace it.
Transfer Assessments and Supplementary Measures. Each Party shall provide the other with information reasonably necessary to evaluate a Restricted Transfer and document any transfer impact or risk assessment required by Data Protection Laws. The Parties shall implement supplementary contractual, technical, or organizational measures where reasonably necessary to maintain an appropriate level of protection.
Successor Transfer Mechanisms. If a new or revised lawful transfer mechanism becomes available or legally required, SambaNova may update the applicable transfer terms upon reasonable prior written notice, provided that update is reasonably necessary to address the legal change and does not materially reduce the protection afforded to Customer Personal Data. No update may modify the EU SCCs or UK Addendum except as permitted by those instruments.
Data Protection Impact Assessment and Prior Consultation. Taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information available to SambaNova, SambaNova shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer with data protection impact assessments, risk assessments, transfer assessments, and prior consultations with competent authorities that Data Protection Laws require in connection with SambaNova’s Processing of Customer Personal Data. Such assistance shall include providing information concerning the nature, scope, context, purposes, security, and risks of the Processing to the extent reasonably available to SambaNova.
Subprocessors.
General Authorization. Customer provides SambaNova with general written authorization to engage the Subprocessors identified on SambaNova’s then-current Subprocessor list, initially set forth in Annex III and thereafter maintained at https://trust.sambanova.ai/subprocessors (the “Subprocessor List”). SambaNova shall provide Customer with at least thirty (30) days’ prior written notice before adding or replacing a Subprocessor, including notice through an email or subscription mechanism made available with the Subprocessor List, except that if SambaNova reasonably believes engaging a new Subprocessor on an expedited basis is necessary to protect the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of the Customer Personal Data or avoid material disruption to the Services, SambaNova will instead give such notice as soon as reasonably practicable.
Subprocessor Obligations. Before a Subprocessor Processes Customer Personal Data, SambaNova shall enter into a written agreement imposing, in substance, the same data-protection obligations imposed on SambaNova under this DPA to the extent applicable to the Subprocessor’s services. SambaNova remains responsible for the performance of each Subprocessor’s data-protection obligations to the same extent SambaNova would be responsible if it performed the Processing itself.
Objections. Customer may object to the appointment of a new or replacement Subprocessor on reasonable grounds relating to data protection by providing written notice within fifteen (15) calendar days of receipt of notice from SambaNova. The notice must describe Customer’s reasonable grounds for the objection and the Parties shall discuss such concerns in good faith with a view to achieving a commercially reasonable resolution. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection, SambaNova may elect not to use the proposed Subprocessor for Customer Personal Data, or either Party may terminate only the affected Services in accordance with the Agreement.
Return or Deletion of Personal Data. Upon the expiration or termination of the Services involving Processing of Customer Personal Data, SambaNova shall, at Customer’s choice, return or securely delete Customer Personal Data and delete existing copies, unless applicable law requires retention. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, SambaNova shall complete the return or deletion from the active systems within thirty (30) days and delete remaining copies from backups in accordance with its ordinary backup cycle, not to exceed ninety (90) days. If applicable law requires retention, SambaNova shall, unless legally prohibited, notify Customer of the requirement and shall retain the Customer Personal Data only for the legally required period and purpose. Upon Customer’s written request, SambaNova shall certify completion of the required deletion.
Security
Security Measures. SambaNova shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect Customer Personal Data in its control from Security Incidents and to preserve the security and confidentiality of the Customer Personal Data, taking into account the state of the art and industry best practices, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of Processing (“Security Measures”). Such Security Measures shall include, at a minimum, those measures described in Annex II of this DPA. SambaNova shall ensure that any person who is authorized by SambaNova to Process Customer Personal Data under this DPA shall be under an appropriate obligation of confidentiality (whether a contractual or statutory duty).
Updates to Security Measures. Customer acknowledges that the Security Measures are subject to technical progress and development and that SambaNova may update or modify the Security Measures from time to time, provided that such updates and modifications do not result in the degradation of the overall level of security applicable to Customer Personal Data during the term of the affected Services.
Customer Security Responsibilities. Customer shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect Customer Personal Data under its control from Security Incidents and to preserve the security and confidentiality of such Customer Personal Data. Customer is responsible for (i) protecting the security of all Customer credentials used to access the Services; (ii) securing the Services and any Customer System (with such steps to include, without limitation, the regular rotation of access keys and other industry standard steps to preclude unauthorized access); (iii) backing up and securing Customer Personal Data under Customer’s control within the Services or other Customer-controlled system; and (iv) reviewing the information made available by SambaNova relating to data security and privacy and making an independent determination as to whether the Services meet Customer’s requirements and legal obligations under applicable Data Protection Laws. Customer’s responsibilities do not limit SambaNova’s obligations under this DPA.
Security Incident Response. To the extent required by applicable Data Protection Laws, upon becoming aware of a Security Incident, SambaNova shall notify Customer without undue delay. Notice shall be provided to Customer’s designated notice contact under the Agreement, and shall include: (i) the nature of the Security Incident; (ii) where possible, the categories and approximate numbers of the affected Data Subjects and Customer Personal Data records; (iii) the likely consequences of the Security Incident; (iv) the measures taken or proposed to contain, investigate, remediate, and mitigate the Security Incident; and (v) contact information through which Customer may obtain additional information. Where the information cannot be provided at the same time, SambaNova may provide it in phases without undue further delay. SambaNova shall promptly take reasonable steps within its control to contain, investigate, remediate, and mitigate the Security Incident and shall reasonably assist Customer with legally required notifications to authorities and affected individuals. SambaNova shall not notify any third party of a Security Incident identifying Customer without Customer’s prior written approval, except as required by law. Notification does not constitute an admission of fault or liability. The Parties shall reasonably cooperate in investigating a Security Incident. The obligations set forth herein shall not apply to Security Incidents to the extent they are caused by Customer or its Authorized Users.
Audit. Upon the reasonable request of the Customer, and at Customer’s expense and no more than once per year, SambaNova shall make available to Customer all information in its possession reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations described in this DPA and shall allow for, and cooperate with, reasonable assessments by Customer or the Customer’s designated assessor. Customer shall not use such an audit report for any other purpose than to assess SambaNova’s compliance with this DPA. Customer shall have the right, upon notice, to take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate SambaNova’s unauthorized use of Customer Personal Data. Customer shall bear its audit costs unless the audit identifies a material breach of this DPA by SambaNova, in which case SambaNova shall bear its reasonable costs of responding to the audit and remediating the breach. Nothing in this Section limits a competent authority’s audit or inspection rights or Customer’s rights under the EU SCCs or UK Addendum. Where required by U.S. Data Protection Laws, Customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to verify that SambaNova’s Processing is consistent with Customer’s legal obligations.
General Terms
Termination and Survival. This DPA and all provisions herein shall remain in effect so long as the Agreement is in effect.
Ineffective Clause. If individual provisions of this DPA are or become ineffective, the effectiveness of the remaining provisions shall not be affected. The Parties shall replace the ineffective clause with a legally allowed clause, which will accomplish the intended commercial intention as closely as possible.
Conflicts. In case of contradictions between this DPA and the provisions of the Agreement, the provisions of this DPA shall prevail. For a Restricted Transfer, the applicable EU SCCs or UK Addendum prevail over this DPA and the Agreement to the extent of a conflict. Except to the extent prohibited by Data Protection Laws or inconsistent with the EU SCCs or UK Addendum, the exclusions and limitations of liability in the Agreement apply to this DPA. Nothing in the Agreement or this DPA limits the rights of Data Subjects or competent authorities or modifies liability imposed by the EU SCCs, UK Addendum, or other nonwaivable requirements of Data Protection Laws.
Dispute Resolution. Before commencing any legal proceeding arising out of or relating to this DPA, the Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute through negotiation between senior representatives of each Party with authority to settle the matter, for a period of not less than thirty (30) days following written notice of the dispute, unless a Party reasonably believes that interim injunctive relief is necessary to prevent irreparable harm, in which case this Section 12(d) shall not prevent that Party from seeking such relief. Any discussions conducted pursuant to this Section 12(d) shall be treated as confidential settlement negotiations and shall not be used as evidence in any subsequent proceeding. This Section does not restrict a Party’s obligations concerning a Security Incident, regulatory inquiry, legally required suspension of Processing, or Restricted Transfer, and does not limit the rights of Data Subjects or competent authorities under Data Protection Laws, the EU SCCs, or UK Addendum.
Amendment. Except for updates expressly permitted by Sections 6, 8, and 10, this DPA may be amended only by a written agreement between the Parties. SambaNova may make an amendment reasonably necessary to comply with a change in Data Protection Laws upon prior written notice, provided that the amendment does not materially reduce the protection afforded to Customer Personal Data or alter the Parties’ commercial rights beyond what the legal change reasonably requires.
Governing Law and Jurisdiction. Except as otherwise required by the EU SCCs, UK Addendum, or applicable Data Protection Laws, the governing law and jurisdiction as set forth in the Agreement apply to this DPA.

The Parties shall use the official, unmodified text of the applicable Module. The selections and Appendix information are completed through Section 6 and Annexes I-III of this DPA.

Clause 1
Purpose and scope
(a) The purpose of these standard contractual clauses is to ensure compliance with the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (General Data Protection Regulation) (1) for the transfer of data to a third country.
(b) The Parties:
(i) the natural or legal person(s), public authority/ies, agency/ies or other body/ies (hereinafter ‘entity/ies’) transferring the personal data, as listed in Annex I.A (hereinafter each ‘data exporter’), and

have agreed to these standard contractual clauses (hereinafter: ‘Clauses’).
(c) These Clauses apply with respect to the transfer of personal data as specified in Annex I.B.
(d) The Appendix to these Clauses containing the Annexes referred to therein forms an integral part of these Clauses.

Clause 2
Effect and invariability of the Clauses
(a) These Clauses set out appropriate safeguards, including enforceable data subject rights and effective legal remedies, pursuant to Article 46(1) and Article 46(2)(c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and, with respect to data transfers from controllers to processors and/or processors to processors, standard contractual clauses pursuant to Article 28(7) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, provided they are not modified, except to select the appropriate Module(s) or to add or update information in the Appendix. This does not prevent the Parties from including the standard contractual clauses laid down in these Clauses in a wider contract and/or to add other clauses or additional safeguards, provided that they do not contradict, directly or indirectly, these Clauses or prejudice the fundamental rights or freedoms of data subjects.
(b) These Clauses are without prejudice to obligations to which the data exporter is subject by virtue of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
Clause 3

(iii) Clause 9(a), (c), (d) and (e);

(vi) Clause 15.1(c), (d) and (e);
(vii) Clause 16(e);
(viii) Clause 18(a) and (b).
(b) Paragraph (a) is without prejudice to rights of data subjects under Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
Clause 4
Interpretation
(a) Where these Clauses use terms that are defined in Regulation (EU) 2016/679, those terms shall have the same meaning as in that Regulation.
(b) These Clauses shall be read and interpreted in the light of the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
(c) These Clauses shall not be interpreted in a way that conflicts with rights and obligations provided for in Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
Clause 5
Hierarchy
In the event of a contradiction between these Clauses and the provisions of related agreements between the Parties, existing at the time these Clauses are agreed or entered into thereafter, these Clauses shall prevail.

SECTION II – OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES
Clause 8
Data protection safeguards
The data exporter warrants that it has used reasonable efforts to determine that the data importer is able, through the implementation of appropriate technical and organizational measures, to satisfy its obligations under these Clauses.
8.1 Instructions
(a) The data importer shall process the personal data only on documented instructions from the data exporter. The data exporter may give such instructions throughout the duration of the contract.
(b) The data importer shall immediately inform the data exporter if it is unable to follow those instructions.
8.2 Purpose limitation
The data importer shall process the personal data only for the specific purpose(s) of the transfer, as set out in Annex I.B, unless on further instructions from the data exporter.
8.3 Transparency
On request, the data exporter shall make a copy of these Clauses, including the Appendix as completed by the Parties, available to the data subject free of charge. To the extent necessary to protect business secrets or other confidential information, including the measures described in Annex II and personal data, the data exporter may redact part of the text of the Appendix to these Clauses prior to sharing a copy, but shall provide a meaningful summary where the data subject would otherwise not be able to understand the its content or exercise his/her rights. On request, the Parties shall provide the data subject with the reasons for the redactions, to the extent possible without revealing the redacted information. This Clause is without prejudice to the obligations of the data exporter under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
8.4 Accuracy
If the data importer becomes aware that the personal data it has received is inaccurate, or has become outdated, it shall inform the data exporter without undue delay. In this case, the data importer shall cooperate with the data exporter to erase or rectify the data.
8.5 Duration of processing and erasure or return of data
Processing by the data importer shall only take place for the duration specified in Annex I.B. The data importer shall also notify the data exporter without undue delay after having become aware of the breach. Such notification shall contain the details of a contact point where more information can be obtained, a description of the nature of the breach (including, where possible, categories and approximate number of data subjects and personal data records concerned), its likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects. Where, and in so far as, it is not possible to provide all information at the same time, the initial notification shall contain the information then available and further information shall, as it becomes available, subsequently be provided without undue delay.
(d) The data importer shall cooperate with and assist the data exporter to enable the data exporter to comply with its obligations under Regulation (EU) 2016/679, in particular to notify the competent supervisory authority and the affected data subjects, taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to the data importer.

Logging and monitoring — audit trails of who accessed what, when
Network security — firewalls, intrusion detection, segmentation
Vulnerability management — patching cadence, penetration testing, vulnerability scanning
Backup and resilience — the ability to restore availability and access in a timely manner after an incident (this phrase is lifted almost directly from Art. 32(1)(c))
Testing and evaluation — a process for regularly testing and assessing the effectiveness of the measures (Art. 32(1)(d))
Organisational measures — the people/process controls:
Access governance — onboarding/offboarding procedures, periodic access reviews
Staff training and confidentiality — employees under confidentiality obligations, trained on data handling
Incident response plan — a documented breach notification process with defined timelines (GDPR requires notifying the controller “without undue delay”)
Vendor/Subprocessor management — due diligence and contractual flow-down obligations to any Subprocessors
Data minimisation by design — policies limiting what's collected/retained to what's necessary
Physical security — controls over data centers/facilities (often inherited from cloud infra provider)
Governance documentation — a named security point of contact, a security policy that's reviewed periodically, and audit rights for the controller (or reliance on third-party audit reports)
